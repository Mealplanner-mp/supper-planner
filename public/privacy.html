Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

Plan to Dish ("we," "us") provides a meal-planning web app at plantodish.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email, username, and password. Authentication is handled by Supabase; we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Payment information — subscription payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number — only your subscription status, plan, and a Stripe customer reference.
  • Content you provide — recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry staples, and dietary preferences you enter into the app.
  • AI assistant data — if you use the AI cooking assistant, recipe search, or recipe upload features, your question or recipe content is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use those features.
  • Support communications — if you email support@plantodish.com, we keep that correspondence to help you and to improve the app.

How we use your information

  • To operate the app — save your data, generate meal plans, and keep you signed in
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To respond to support requests
  • To send essential account emails (password resets, payment confirmations)
  • Occasionally, to send product updates or promotions by email — you can unsubscribe from these at any time via the link in the email itself

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use the following service providers to run the app, each of whom only receives what they need to do their job:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Anthropic — AI assistant responses (only when you use that feature)
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Zoho — our support email inbox
  • Brevo — sending occasional product update emails

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to save your AI assistant chat history on your own device, so it's there next time you open the app. This data stays in your browser and isn't shared with anyone beyond what's described above.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email support@plantodish.com.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time — most of this is available directly in the app's Account page, or by contacting us at the email above.

Children's privacy

Plan to Dish is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. (Our "baby-friendly planning" feature helps parents plan meals — it doesn't involve collecting any information from a child.)

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our database. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@plantodish.com.
